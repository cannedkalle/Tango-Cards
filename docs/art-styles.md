# House art style

**Playform Kids** is the single active style. Follow [the canonical rules](visual-style.md) and [the construction brief](art-direction-prompt.md).

Warm geometric characters and recognizable play objects bring the friendliness; restrained typography, spacing and controls bring consistency. The historical Paper Toy Index, realistic generated previews and adult Playform icon study are retired directions. Existing old image files are retained for history, not referenced by production UI.
