All **93 requested candidates are accepted**, with narrower labels and date ranges where needed. The 2007 entry is named **iPhone**; the 1994 entry is named **Early smartphone (IBM Simon)** to keep their milestones distinct. The playable pool grows from 224 to **317 items**.

Reviewed for recognizable identity, duplicate milestones, neutral child-friendly definitions, date evidence, ambiguity, and playable comparisons. Original ancestors, prototype dates, and wider adoption are distinguished in each entry’s notes. A brand label identifies the dated milestone without claiming it invented the whole category. The user explicitly requested every candidate, so named snacks and toy lines are retained despite the taxonomy’s usual preference for generic formats.

Sources are linked per entry below and bundled in the game. Most are museums, libraries, universities, standards bodies, patents, or the creator/manufacturer. The skatepark entry uses local historical reporting and makes no world-first claim. Dates are historical milestones, not uniformly invention dates: inventions, discoveries, publications, launches, openings, and first uses are deliberately labeled.

Added 186 selected Curated comparisons, giving each new item at least two appearances. Explicit comparison tags link related concepts that cross the existing categories, such as railways and toy trains, eyewear, and weather gear. Wild mode includes every non-overlapping chronology.
