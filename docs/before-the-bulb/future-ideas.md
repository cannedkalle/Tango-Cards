# Future iteration ideas

These features are explicitly outside the MVP. Revisit them after families have tried the core guessing loop.

- Manual Easy / Tricky / Super Tricky levels and age or category selection.
- Adaptive difficulty based on answers, familiarity, or category performance.
- Smarter selection that balances categories and avoids repeated objects across adjacent pairs.
- Car-ride mode with browser text-to-speech, followed later by audio-only play or optional voice input.
- Saved progress and seen-question history across sessions.
- Multiple verified facts per object with fact rotation.
- Animated historical timelines and a browsable invention collection.
- Streaks, achievements, daily challenges, and tournaments.
- Player profiles and structured multiplayer family sessions.
- Additional languages and user-created collections.
- Installable offline play and offline relaunch.
- Expansion to 300–500+ verified objects after the research and review workflow is reliable.

Stable object and pair IDs are sufficient preparation now. Do not add unused settings, placeholder controls, statistics, or backend services for these ideas during the MVP build.
